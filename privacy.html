Privacy Policy

How we collect, use and protect your data

Last updated: 2026-09-09

1. Who we are

ANMO HOME APPLIANCES ("we", "us") operates this website to provide home appliance wholesale, OEM and export services. This policy explains how we collect, use and protect your personal data.

2. Data we collect

When you submit an inquiry or place an order, we collect: your name, company, email, phone/WhatsApp number, country, message content and any files you attach. We also collect basic analytics data (pages visited, device type) if you consent to cookies.

3. How we use your data

We use your data to respond to inquiries, prepare quotes and orders, provide after-sales support, and improve our website. We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

4. Cookies

This website uses essential cookies for basic functionality and, with your consent, analytics cookies to understand traffic. You can accept or decline non-essential cookies at any time using the banner.

5. Data sharing

We may share your data with payment providers (PayPal, banks) and logistics partners only as required to complete your order.

6. Data retention & security

We keep inquiry and order data for as long as needed to serve you and meet legal obligations, and we apply reasonable technical measures to protect it.

7. Your rights

You may request access, correction or deletion of your personal data at any time by contacting us at the email address on this website.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy? Email us at the address shown on our Contact page.
